# Board theme attribution

Every board texture here comes from
[lichess-org/lila](https://github.com/lichess-org/lila) (`public/images/board/`), by the
lila authors and [pirouetti](https://lichess.org/@/pirouetti), licensed **AGPLv3+** (see
lila's [COPYING.md](https://github.com/lichess-org/lila/blob/master/COPYING.md)). These
are the 15 themes lichess marks as featured, minus their 3D-only ones. `wood4` is
Endgame Nirvana's default.

The only change made is re-encoding: the JPEG sources were converted to WebP at
1024×1024, quality 80, and the flat-colour PNG sources to lossless WebP — together
cutting ~853 KB down to ~313 KB. `newspaper.svg` is copied verbatim.
