# Piece set attribution

All piece sets are taken unmodified from [lichess-org/lila](https://github.com/lichess-org/lila)
(`public/piece/`). Licences below are as recorded in lila's
[COPYING.md](https://github.com/lichess-org/lila/blob/master/COPYING.md).

Only sets whose licence permits redistribution are included. Lichess's `alpha`
("free for personal non commercial use") and `companion` ("freeware") sets are
deliberately **not** bundled, since neither grants a redistribution right.
